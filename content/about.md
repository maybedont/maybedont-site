# About Maybe Don't, AI

We're building the common sense guardrails that AI agents need.

As AI assistants become more capable and autonomous, they need guardrails to prevent unintended consequences. Maybe Don't provides real-time monitoring and intelligent blocking for AI tool calls, giving organizations the confidence to deploy AI agents safely.

## Our Mission

To mitigate risk when using agentic AI in high risk areas of your business.
